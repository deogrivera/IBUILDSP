Uploading images to Google Drive

  1. 1AirDrop the images from your mobile device to the MacBook.
  2. 2Open Google Drive — our image repository.
  3. 3Select the specific Subproject (SP) Name folder.
  4. 4Make a specific folder named after the activity conducted (e.g. the JIT or field monitoring visit).
  5. 5Upload the images into that folder.
  6. 6Copy the folder's share link and paste it into the Photo link field when entering data in this app.
