02

Where the money is versus where the work is

The core PRDP monitoring question: is billing keeping pace with construction?

Physical Accomplishment vs. Financial Status, by subproject

Blue bars are % of construction actually done on-site; amber bars are % of contract value billed/paid. When blue runs well ahead of amber, the contractor has out-built the billing — usually fine, just billing lag. When amber is higher than blue, flag it for review (advance/mobilization payments running ahead of work).

Status mix

Share of subprojects that are Completed, On-going, or awaiting Notice of Location (NOL1) / not yet started. A large "with NOL1" slice signals a pipeline bottleneck upstream of construction.

Subproject type mix — investment

Total PRDP counterpart cost grouped by infrastructure type. Shows where capital is concentrated: cold chain / warehousing (VCRI), roads (FMR), or bridges.

03

Fund utilization pipeline

Counterpart cost → Obligated → Disbursed. Gaps show where funds are stuck.

Counterpart cost, obligation, and disbursement — per subproject

Each subproject shows three bars: total PRDP counterpart cost (the ceiling), amount obligated (funds set aside via ASA/ORS), and amount actually disbursed (paid out). A wide gap between "Obligated" and "Disbursed" on a subproject means the paperwork is done but the money hasn't moved — a candidate for expediting.

Disbursement rate, ranked lowest to highest

Disbursement ÷ obligation, per subproject. The lowest bars are where cash is obligated but not yet moving — prioritize these for site visits and billing follow-up.

LP vs. GOP funding split

Loan Proceeds (LP) vs. GOP counterpart share of total obligation. PRDP's standard split is roughly 90% LP / 10% GOP — this checks the portfolio against that ratio.

Undisbursed vs. unobligated balance

Undisbursed = obligated but not yet paid out (near-term, expect movement soon). Unobligated = not yet even set aside (needs an ASA/ORS before anything else can happen). Unobligated balances are the more urgent bottleneck to clear.

Contract cost by subproject

Ranks subprojects by total contract value, largest first. Useful for prioritizing monitoring effort toward the biggest financial exposure.
